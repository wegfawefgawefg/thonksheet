# The quick map for one setting (scripts/oneset.sh). `row NAME: flags` is a world; every
# other line `NAME: flags` is a column (a setting run unchanged on every row). Every row runs
# on a half-length life (--ticks 20 --sensor-side 3 --motor-side 3 --steps 20000 --window 1000;
# the standard 40,000 halved to fit the time budget: the output rule on seeds 1-4 scores
# within 0.05 of its 40,000-step score on every row)
# with its own flags after that; a row's flags are the world's own (layout, lines, buttons).
# Results: docs/results-2026-11-26-one-setting.md.
