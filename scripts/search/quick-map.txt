# The quick map: the worlds every candidate of the search is scored on (docs/search.md).
# Tiers 1 to 4 of docs/task-map.md as they stood on main on 2026-11-25.
#
# The budget: seconds of one core for one pass of every world on one seed, on this PC.
# Measured 2026-11-25 with this PC at load 37 on 32 threads (about half speed): full
# engine 184 s (13.5 s a life world, 2 s a probe), table 351 s, minimal 53 s. Quiet, about
# half that. `scripts/search.sh report` shows what a pass really costs, per world, engine
# and machine, and marks a map over budget. Adding a world means raising this on purpose
# or cutting one.
budget   200

# every life: 10,000 steps (20 ticks a step from the plug), windows of 200; late = the
# last tenth. No coin or twin inside the call: the twin is its own cached run.
life     --steps 10000 --window 200 --no-baselines --alive-ticks 0 --rhythm-ticks 0
# every probe (full engine only): the sheet as born, learning off
probe    --tries 1500 --settle 5000
select   1 2 3
confirm  5 6 7 8
pass     0.3
timeout  900
