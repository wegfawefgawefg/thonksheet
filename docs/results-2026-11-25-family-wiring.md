# Wiring at birth (2026-11-25)

Ideas 19, 20, 21, 77, 78, 79, 80, 83, plus "kinds by place". Each is a `wire_*` genome number
(`src/blob/birth_shape.cpp`); all 0 runs the old `wire()` and is byte-identical (an echo life,
a readout life, the xor feature probe and the carry probe, csv diff). Birth only: slots, words
and the tick are unchanged; the tick does the same work whatever the masks hold. A shaped
neuron keeps today's expected count of local connections. `blobprobe wiring` shows what each
does to the wiring and whether the sheet stays alive. Ideas 81 and 82 are growth-time rules,
not birth wiring: not done here.

## The mechanisms

- layers (`wire_layers`): bits split in, middle, out (sensor cells in, motor cells out). In to
  middle, middle to middle and out inside a tile, out to the next tile's in and to far: 2^L
  likelier.
- fine locality (`wire_fine`): the chance halves every 4/S cells.
- fewer slots (`wire_slots`): own tile plus S-1 drawn neighbour tiles, same count.
- mirror (`wire_mirror_percent`): where A hears B, B adds A with this chance, then drops
  unreturned links back to its count. 100 makes every local link reciprocal.
- hubs (`wire_hub_per_1024`, `wire_hub_shift`): hubs hear 2^s times as many and are 2^s
  likelier as sources.
- streams (`wire_stream`, `_dir`, `_turn`): S doublings per 8 cells upstream. Dir 0 flows +x
  (away from the probes' cue along x), 4 flows +y (input area toward output area), 10 toward
  the cue corner.
- far topography (`wire_far_topo`, `_jitter`): 1 a tile's far links all land on one tile per
  slot; 2 one shift per sector (a map).
- inhibitory reach (`wire_inhib_reach`): >0 inhibitory neurons listen wider; <0 inhibition
  stays local (inhibitory neurons favour their own tile, and are 2^S less likely as sources in
  neighbour tiles).
- two sheets (`wire_two_sheets`): no local link across the middle row; that percent of the
  bottom half's far slots are fed from the same place on the top half (the sensor half); the
  bottom sends back to 1 in 8 of the top half's far links.

## Reading of the screen

- Most of the family is null or harmful. Layers, fine locality, fewer slots and hubs cost
  "which" and order roughly in step with strength. Far topography is null.
- Inhibition kept local (`wire_inhib_reach -3`) is the largest mover: gap 1.60 to 4.62 (t
  +12), exclusive-or 1.76 to 2.84 (t +8), at a cost to which (-1.5), order (-0.7) and carry3
  (-0.28). Gap at that level matches what long delays give.
- Mirror links help steadily with strength: mirror 100 gap +0.66, xor +0.24, carry3 +0.34
  (t +7.7), no loss elsewhere beyond noise.
- Streams carry along their direction: stream 2 carry3 +0.43 and reach +0.75; turning
  stream 2 carry3 +0.57. Flowing toward the cue (stream 2 back) everything drops. Strong
  streams wreck xor and gap. A stream's gain depends on where the input sits relative to the
  flow (geometry, not cue identity).
- Two sheets: reach 3.25 to 5.0 on all four seeds, carry3 +0.22, features unchanged. The
  topographic forward feed relays the cue into the second half.
- Learning erases birth wiring fast: `blobprobe wiring --g.wire_mirror_percent 50 --age
  100000` takes reciprocity 0.80 back to 0.15 within 100,000 ticks (one seed).
